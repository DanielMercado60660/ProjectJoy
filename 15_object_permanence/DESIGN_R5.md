# Round 5 — bind an identity, retain alternatives, spend one look

**Status:** design/code handoff; learned outcomes **not measured**. Earlier exhibit stays intact.

**Hypotheses.** (H1) Explicit frame-0 binding reduces the Gaussian recipe's visible error, especially at tiny initial x-gaps. (H2) Three full-covariance components improve hidden-frame NLL/calibration over one Gaussian. (H3) A causal uncertainty-triggered look improves subsequent NLL/error more than a random look at identical cost. Failure to beat a well-tuned physics-aware filter is an informative result.

**Methods and information.** Compare cue-conditioned CNN–GRU Gaussian, identical trunk with K=3 mixture head, cue-initialized bouncing Kalman, and a joint particle filter. All receive identical RGB views, present-slot mask, wall geometry, and per-slot frame-0 position plus independent σ=0.25 px noise. These cues are an explicit additional sensor, not inferred perfect detections; no later ground-truth state enters tracking. The neural pair shares the existing `hard/final` warm start, identical batches/peeks, optimizer recipe, and completed-step count. Cue projections start at zero; mixture components copy the Gaussian head with symmetric ±0.5 px offsets. Report the common pretraining separately from fine-tuning compute. The PF knows elastic collisions, boundary reflections and the 0.3 hidden-entry turn probability; it marginalizes detection assignments and uses an importance-corrected frame-1 velocity proposal. Its output is a particle KDE, with finite-particle depletion explicitly monitored.

**Look protocol.** Training: random one-wall transparency in 2/3 of sequences, otherwise never. Test policies: never; uniform random time/wall; first threshold crossing of the tracker's posterior spread weighted by wall probability, choosing the highest-scoring wall. Decisions for time t use only observations through t−1. The threshold is that tracker's validation 75th percentile; a forced look at frame 31 prevents free abstention. Both look policies spend exactly one view in frames 4–31. Physics continues unchanged. Ordinary and revealed frames share sensor noise; masks for hidden/reappearance scoring remain the natural, opaque-view masks.

**Tuning and splits.** Read existing `hard_train`; baseline screening on `hard_val[0:8]`, three finalists on `[64:88]`; thresholds use `[128:144]` (battery: four sequences). Kalman grid: q={.035,.15,.5,1.2}, r={.07,.5,2,7,14,30}. PF grid: q={.02,.15,.5}, r={.3,1,3,6}, miss={.05,.2,.35}, KDE σ={.5,1.5,4,8}, 256 particles (512 on AC). Selection is validation all-frame NLL; every tried configuration and incomplete search is recorded. Review boundary optima and ESS before testing. Headline seeds are **51034000+i**; reserved smoke seeds **51024000+i** never contribute. Old `hard_test` is secondary. No model selection on either test.

**Metrics.** Pixel mean error (visible/hidden/reappearance, plus initial x-gap strata); unclipped mixture NLL; Rosenblatt x-then-y coverage at nominal 0.393/0.865; identity-confusion entries/sequence and final swaps (legacy permutation metric, 2 px margin). Peek value: same-frame spread reduction versus ordinary observation, plus paired subsequent error/NLL/confusion/final-swap changes. Retain sequence rows and bootstrap CIs. Save sequence 0 and the least-helpful learned look, including mixture components. H1 is a remeasurement: this budget does not include a matched cue-free fine-tuning control, so improvement over the old exhibit cannot be attributed solely to cues.
