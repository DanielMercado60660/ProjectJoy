Balls roll behind grey walls. A CNN+GRU tracker (left) and a Kalman filter with a colour detector (right) watch the same frames and report a position and an uncertainty ellipse (1σ solid, 2σ faint) for every ball, including the ones they can't see. The dotted ring is the true hidden position. In shell-game mode the replay stops while a ball is hidden. Click where you think it is, then compare your guess with the two trackers.
