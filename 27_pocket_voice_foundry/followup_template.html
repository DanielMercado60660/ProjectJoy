Pocket Voice Foundry follow-up: blind listening

Each group plays one sentence through several systems under letters shuffled afresh on every page load. Mark the one you prefer in each group (or rate several), then press Show my results at the bottom: it reveals every label and counts your picks per system, as text you can copy. Clips are loudness-matched to -23 LUFS, resampled to 16 kHz and embedded as 96 kbps MP3, so the page works offline. In RESULTS.md, listener preference is "not measured" until someone sends their picks.
