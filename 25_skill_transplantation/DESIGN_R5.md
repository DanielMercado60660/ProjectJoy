**PPO changes, fixed before new tests.** Match the working earlier PPO's 16 environments × 256 rollout, 10 epochs, minibatch 1024, learning rate 3e-4, per-minibatch advantage normalization, reward scale 0.1, and no explicit entropy bonus. This avoids transferring an already collapsed exploration distribution. No claim these fixes are proven until the runner returns results.
