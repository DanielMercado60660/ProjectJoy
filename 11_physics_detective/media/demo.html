The Physics Detective

A block sits on a table. Its kinetic friction μk is hidden somewhere between 0.05 and 0.8 (static friction is 1.2 × μk). You choose how hard and how long to push; a noisy camera (±8 cm) films the first metre of the table. Too gentle and nothing moves, too hard and the block slides out of view. Pin down μk with as few pushes as you can, then make your accusation.

    Accusation

    You win if you're within 0.01 of the truth. For comparison, over 500 simulated cases the information-greedy detective (the one behind the button) was within 0.01 for good after a median of 1 push (90% by push 2, every case by push 4). Random pushing needed a median of 4, and 3% of cases still weren't solved after 15.
