Tiny Grand Prix

Small neural networks (13→32→32→2, ~1.6k weights) that learned to race from 10 wall-distance rays and their own speed. Every track here was held out from training. Everything runs live in this page: same physics and policy weights as the Python code (JS computes in float64, Python's policy in float32, so lap times can differ by a step or two). Try lowering the grip: the grip-randomized policies (off by default, tick them below) keep going long after the others crash.

Keyboard car: ↑/W throttle, ↓/S brake, ←→/A D steer. Touch: hold left/right half of the track view to steer, it throttles by itself.

Race setup

Training grip was 1.0. Lower grip = icier: the car slides wide if it enters corners too fast.
