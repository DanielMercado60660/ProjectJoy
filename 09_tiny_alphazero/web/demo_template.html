How this works

The network sees the board as two 6×7 planes (its stones, your stones) and outputs a probability for each column (the prior) plus a value in [−1, 1] (who is winning). Monte Carlo tree search uses the prior to decide which moves to explore and the value instead of random playouts; the move played is the most-visited column. "policy only" skips search and just plays the network's top prior. Weights were exported from the MLX checkpoint with BatchNorm folded into the convolutions.
