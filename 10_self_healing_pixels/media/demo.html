Self-Healing Pixels

A neural cellular automaton: every pixel runs the same tiny neural network and sees only its 3×3 neighbourhood. Drag across the creature to erase cells and watch it grow back.

Models: regeneration-trained saw circular wounds during training; growth-only was trained with the same sample pool and budget but was never damaged. Half-cuts and freehand strokes were never used in training. Update rule: identity + Sobel perception (48 numbers) → 128 ReLU → 16-channel residual update, applied to a random 50% of cells each step; cells with no living neighbour (alpha > 0.1) are cleared.
