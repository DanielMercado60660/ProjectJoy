Vocoder Thunderdome, round 6

The same fixed 80-band log-mel (16 kHz, n_fft 1024, hop 256), computed from a real recording, goes into each vocoder. Per clip you hear, in shuffled order: the recording, Griffin–Lim (64 iterations), the old non-adversarial neural vocoder that project 27 uses, the same generator trained further on full LJSpeech without a GAN (equal steps), and the round-6 GAN vocoder. Every version is loudness-matched to −23 LUFS. Held-out LJSpeech chapters (LJ045–LJ050) plus two clips from LibriTTS-R speakers no model trained on.
